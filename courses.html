Money Mission

What's Inside the
Complete Course

Nine modules covering every area of personal finance a veteran needs to master — built with CFEI frameworks and veteran-specific context. 34 lessons in all.

01

Foundations & Mindset

3 Lessons

Money mindset, your money story, and the full financial picture — net worth, income, and benefits.

02

Budgeting

4 Lessons

What a budget really is, the methods (50/30/20, zero-based, envelope), building one from scratch, and making it stick.

03

Understanding Debt

4 Lessons

How debt works, the true cost of debt, taking a debt inventory, and military-specific situations (SCRA, MLA, predatory lenders).

04

Debt Payoff

4 Lessons

The snowball method, the avalanche method, a side-by-side comparison, and negotiating and managing debt.

05

Credit

7 Lessons

Credit basics, how scores are calculated, reading your reports, building and rebuilding credit, disputing errors, and identity protection.

06

Saving

3 Lessons

Why savings comes first, building your emergency fund, and savings goals beyond the emergency fund.

07

Income

3 Lessons

Your full income picture (VA disability, GI Bill, SSDI), employer benefits you may be leaving behind, and increasing your income.

08

Investing & Retirement

3 Lessons

Investing basics, retirement accounts (TSP, BRS, IRA, 401k), building long-term wealth, and breaking the cycle.

09

Your Plan

3 Lessons

Building your personal plan, handling setbacks, and the resources and next steps for what comes after the course.
